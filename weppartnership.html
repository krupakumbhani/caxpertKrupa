CAxpert has been a committed partner of the Women Entrepreneurship Platform (WEP) since 2019, working together to provide crucial compliance support to women entrepreneurs. Through a range of initiatives, this collaboration has helped to break down barriers and empower women-led businesses to thrive.

Even during the challenging period of the Covid-19 pandemic, CAxpert continued to support WEP by offering online capacity building sessions on accounting and compliance topics, ensuring that women entrepreneurs had access to vital information and resources.

Beyond pandemic support, CAxpert has played a crucial role in several key WEP initiatives. They've provided consistent compliance support for the Award to Reward program, ensuring participants understand and adhere to necessary regulations. CAxpert also led a capacity-building workshop for green businesses, helping women entrepreneurs navigate the specific compliance needs of this sector. Most recently, they contributed their expertise to the WEP and Make My Trip cohort for Homestay Owners in the Northeast.

This ongoing partnership demonstrates the positive impact that can be achieved when organizations collaborate to support women entrepreneurs. By providing essential compliance services and training, CAxpert and WEP are fostering an environment where women-led businesses can grow with confidence and contribute to India's economic development.
