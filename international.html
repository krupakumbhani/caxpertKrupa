Our Gamut of Services

All Bookkeeping & Data Entry

We handle all your bookkeeping needs, ensuring every transaction is accurately recorded.

AR & AP

Managing multiple vendors? We book payments, maintain ledgers, process payables, and follow up on receivables after invoicing on your behalf.

Financial Statement Preparation

With your banking and other data we will have your books ready in on time at the end of every financial year in accordance with your CPA’s requests.

Payment reconciliation

Handling multiple payment channels? We ensure that payments received through various avenues or in different currencies are correctly booked and reconciled, so no transaction is left unnoticed.

Inventory reconciliation.

For our clients dealing goods, large or small, we know that inventory management is crucial to your cash flow. Our team ensures that your books align with your physical inventory every month.

Bank reconciliation.

We reconcile your bank and credit card statements monthly to ensure no transaction is missed.

1099 Assistance

We gather vendor W-9s, accurately prepare the 1099s after reconciling with business records. This allows for timely fillings and avoidance of penalties.

Assisted Payroll

We work hand-in-hand with your CPA to ensure payroll is processed accurately and efficiently, integrating their expertise with our hands-on support.

All other regular ledgers

Need to track a loan account? Want a breakdown of your interest expenses for the last quarter or month? It’s your business and your data—just tell us what you need, and we’ll provide the relevant ledgers.

Cash Flow Management

We provide real-time visibility into your cash flow, ensuring you always know where your money is coming from and where it's going. We can even assist with forecasting and budgeting.

Customized Excel Modelling

We love numbers and we love excel. If you don’t need a complete accounting solution but need for us to run some data reconciliation or analysis for you, it can be done.
