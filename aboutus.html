Getting your accounts in order should be easy, convenient and economical

We solve the problem of accounting for entrepreneurs. But it’s a lot more than that. We want to help you really know your numbers so that you can chart your path forward.

What’s in a name?

We know most business reply on Certified Public Accountants (CPAs) or Chartered Accountants (CAs) for everything finance. But compliance has increased and both communities are overwhelmed as they continue to remain small. This is where we come in. We are Xpert accountants who get your books organized and provide a perspective on what's going on with your work. We are CAxpert.

What we do

We provide busineses with solid accounting solutions. Let us do the recording and the math so that you can do what you do best, grow your business.

Who we help

Whether you run a small consultancy business, a large ecommerce operation or manufacture and sell home furnishings, we are here for you.

About The Team

NEHA GOENKA

Founder - Director at Espresso Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

CAxpert was founded by Neha Goenka with a clear vision: to revolutionize accounting services by seamlessly integrating compliance management, financial analysis, and data entry. Drawing on her extensive experience in financial markets, including her tenure at Enam Securities (now Axis Capital), Neha has steered CAxpert to become a trusted partner for clients across multiple nations, including India and the United States.

As the driving force behind global client delivery, Neha ensures that CAxpert consistently exceeds expectations. Her commitment to financial excellence is further underscored by her active involvement in the CFA Society, India.

YASHODHARA BAJORIA

Founder - Director at Espresso Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

Yashodhara, with 20 years of experience in Finance and Investment, is the Co-Founder- Director of Espresso Technologies Pvt. Ltd., operating CAxpert, a customized accounting solutions provider for small businesses. Under her and Neha Goenka's leadership, CAxpert has significantly contributed to numerous businesses' growth locally and globally.

As a long-standing member of Executive Committee of the Women Entrepreneurship Platform (WEP) and devoted propagator of critical mass in women workforce through entrepreneurship, Yashodhara continues to foster partnerships and implement strategic plans that bolster the growth of female-led businesses within the ecosystem.

A B.Sc in Business Management holder from IILM, New Delhi, she's also a Governing Body member of Ficci Ladies Organisation 2023-24, leading the Differently Abled initiative, and National Head for Entrepreneruship this year for CII-Young Indians
