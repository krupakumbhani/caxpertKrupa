About The Team

NEHA GOENKA

Founder - Director at Espresso Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

Neha’s first job was at the broking division of Axis Capital (erstwhile Enam Securities Pvt. Ltd) as an Institutional Equity Sales Associate. During this period, Neha was actively involved in the marketing of IPOs, follow on Offers and rights issues of Air Deccan, Reliance Petroleum, Suzlon Energy, Union Bank of India and many more. She and her team helped raise over Rs, 5000 crores though the placement of shares with domestic and international fund houses such Temasek, GIC etc.

A year later, in 2006, she moved into the Institutional Equity Research Team, as a banking research Analyst, to better understand financial statement analysis and the valuations business. As a Research Associate, Neha worked closely with senior management from across the banking and NBFC spectrum including HDFC Bank, Reliance Capital etc.

Post 2008, Neha has been involved in successfully managing the family office and handling an investment portfolio till 2017. While handling the family office and dealing with freelance accountants, Neha’s key learnings centered around the poor understanding most accountants have in marrying accounting figures with business intelligence and compliance requirements. This was exacerbated by the newly implemented Goods and Services Tax in 2017.

Subsequently, CAxpert was launched in November 2018 with the single aim of providing accounting services as a combined offering where compliance management and financial analysis go hand in hand with data entry.

Neha is an active member of CFA Society, India.

Yashodhara started her career with JM Morgan Stanley, where she worked for 5 years as a member of their Private Wealth Team and was managing an AUM of 100 Crores.

Post that she has had experience working in her family office, where she was heading a team and was controller of finances for various NBFC’s. Her growth came at the back of her experience of not only handling, accounting, cash-flow management and tax reporting, but also strategic investment decisions that aligned with the family goals.

Her exposure to the necessity of accounting and accurate bookkeeping came during her work with Marketesh Trading Pvt. Ltd a NBFC where she needed to look out for clean books of accounts of small and micro entrepreneurs to make the lending process seamless.

It was the unpreparedness and the lack of knowledge among these business owners, that led her to believe that a platform needed to be there for small business owners, that would demystify, accounting and financial information, and act as an enabler to empower business owners rather than be perceived as a deterrent to growth.

Yashodhara has been an active member of Young Indians (Yi) for the last 12 years, which is the youth wing of Confederation of Indian Industry (CII) and has been the past chair for their Delhi Chapter and have been is currently on the National Management Team. She is also a board member of Young FICCI Ladies Organisation (YFLO) Delhi, heading their Sponsorship vertical in 2019-2020.
